HOW TO FLY

Drag the ball and release. Pull down to launch up, or sideways for an angled launch. Keyboard launch: hold Down Arrow, aim with Left/Right, then release Down.

Space: jetpack · A/D or Left/Right: steer. Deployables: 1 thruster · 2 boots · 3 teleporter · 4/5/6 health packs. Tap a deployable slot on mobile or click it on desktop. Each use consumes one item. Escape: menu · R: reset flight.

Bounce off platforms, gather coins and scrap, and return to the garage for upgrades. Your payday arrives after each flight.
