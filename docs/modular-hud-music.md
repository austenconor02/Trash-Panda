# Supplied HUD artwork and exclusive music

The original `desktop_HUD.png` (1672×941) and `Mobile_HUD.png` (941×1672) from `F:\trash_panda_launcher_ref\image assets` are copied unchanged into `public/assets/hud-desktop.png` and `public/assets/hud-mobile.png`. Canvas source rectangles reuse their original frames without regenerating or stretching the whole transparent sheet. `src/hud-layout.ts` places the screen interiors and modular deployable frames; `src/flight-hud.ts` renders the live values.

Desktop: altitude and distance sit at the upper left, followed by fuel, oxygen, health, and collected cash for this flight. **Cash Collected is coins plus platform cash only: `score.collectedCents`. It never uses the calculated payday, stat multipliers, recovery deductions, or bank balance.** Payday calculations remain on the results screen. The right module contains gravity, temperature, and a circular radar. Mobile uses the supplied bottom console, with deployable slots at the top (two rows on narrow screens) and radar at the upper right. Short landscape screens move the radar beside the bottom console. Tap/click buttons align with the art, while directional controls sit above the console.

Fuel, oxygen, and health use clamped animated fills clipped to their inner slots. Text fits its allotted width; numbers and maxima update as capacities or resources change. Damaged health glows and vibrates; below half health it pulses red. Actual suit-adjusted cold exposure grows screen-edge frost and icy health-bar edges. LCD scanlines and a top-to-bottom startup match the tablet menus. The HUD does not reboot between launch, flight, and crash states. Reduced motion uses immediate fills, steady warnings, and a stationary radar. See [scrap/deployables/cold details](scrap-deployables-cold.md).

Before purchase, the radar displays **RADAR OFFLINE** and draws no targets. Purchased tiers preserve stable half coverage, full coverage, and colored platform markers. Its drawing is clipped to the supplied circular screen.

Music now has one physical HTML audio element. It fades out before switching between menu and flight sources, preserves both song positions, and stays silent during aiming. Canceled fade callbacks, delayed play promises, replaced controllers, page departure, and competing tabs are guarded. The earlier isolated gameplay test page was closed during this pass.

Validation is limited to code checks, build/typechecking, and static HUD sheets. The game was not launched or played for this change. `tests/hud.mjs` records Canvas operations against static fixture data, verifies supplied asset hashes and layout bounds, checks resource clipping, and tests all radar tiers. `tests/audio-payday.mjs` uses mocked audio, timers, and tab messaging; it produces no sound. `artifacts/hud/static-preview.html` imports only the HUD and fixture models and draws once, with no Game, Input, simulation loop, or audio. Static screenshots are stored beside it at desktop, portrait-mobile, and short-landscape sizes. Live audio listening and gameplay behavior were not tested.
