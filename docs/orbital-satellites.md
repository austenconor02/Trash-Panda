# Orbital satellites

Earth satellites are scattered around three orbital bands centered at 55,000 ft (+280 world pixels/s), 65,000 ft (-320), and 70,000 ft (+350). Each satellite has a stable individual height offset of up to 4,000 ft, covering 51,000–74,000 ft rather than forming flat rows. Each band uses a large horizontal spacing (1,800 / 2,000 / 2,200 world pixels). Positions depend on simulation time and world coordinates; camera motion never respawns or wraps a satellite. Only nearby satellites are materialized for drawing/collision checks. The 160 x 64 display envelope is roughly 184 x 74 CSS pixels at the current 1.15 camera zoom. Speeds allow a several-second desktop crossing; incoming edge labels appear up to 450 world pixels before the satellite reaches the viewport. Narrow screens have a shorter crossing time, with the same warning distance.

Descending onto the top face salvages a satellite: award exactly 10 debris once, preserve health, start the standard smooth platform rebound, and trigger any owned Fuel Absorber. Its panels separate, sparks scatter, and the broken satellite tumbles downward. Destroyed IDs stay removed for the flight even after their six-second wreck animation expires. The first surface crossed wins, so one high-speed descent cannot also consume a lower platform.

Side hits (including descending side entries), upward hits, and stationary overlaps keep the original hazard behavior: 25 health damage, 25% retained horizontal velocity, and 25% vertical speed redirected downward, with a minimum 140 px/s kick. Pending platform rebound is cleared. Relative swept collision accounts for satellite motion and prevents tunneling. A 1.4-second shared cooldown prevents repeated damage or rewards. Damaging hits flash red and enter the existing crash flow when fatal. Lunar flights have no Earth satellites. Pause freezes orbits, wreckage, and cooldowns; a new flight resets them. Teleport arrival starts a new contact sweep.

Implementation: src/satellites.ts (orbits and swept collision), src/satellite-sprite.ts (sprite and warnings), Player.satelliteImpact (momentum/rebound response), and Game.step (damage/death integration). Settings live in config.satellites.

Artwork: public/assets/orbital-satellite.png, generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The PNG is kept intact; source alpha bounds are measured once on load to crop transparent margins when drawing. A simple code-drawn satellite remains visible if the image fails to load.

Validation: npm.cmd run check and npm.cmd run build. Checks cover all three altitudes, orbital motion, sideways/vertical tunneling, a moving satellite hitting a stationary player, avoidance clearance, 75% momentum loss, minimum downward kick, rebound cancellation, cooldown/reset, health damage, fatal contacts, and Moon exclusion. The Chrome smoke harness tests the loaded transparent sprite, renders each orbital lane and edge warnings, and applies a deterministic collision in a real Game instance (100 to 75 health, 4000 to 1000 horizontal speed, 2000 to -500 vertical speed). It also checks orbital pause and captures artifacts/scenery/satellite-*.png. These are focused functional/visual checks; a full progression/difficulty playtest and listening to the impact sound remain manual.
